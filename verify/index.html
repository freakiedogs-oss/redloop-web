Verify a record

This page checks the signature, checks that what was sent comes from the catalog, and recomputes every verdict from the recorded responses. Its security policy forbids any network request, so your file stays here and the endpoint is never contacted.

It cannot verify that the endpoint was the one in production, that the endpoint did not recognize the probes, the real time of the run, or decoy hits, network failures, status codes and content types (those are RedLoop's own statement). Nor can it tell whether the test agent really answered what the record shows: it checks the record against the rules, not the answer against the agent.

Works without us: download the offline verifier and run node redloop-verify.mjs record.json (Node 22 or later). Keys are inside the file.

The demo stores no canary and no document. The hourly limit uses a salted hash of your network address, rotated daily. RedLoop has no contact channel yet.

RedLoop is an early demo. It makes no statement about compliance with any law or standard.
